# Spaced-Repetition Schedule (Walk & Recall decks)

25 Walk & Recall decks. Each deck is recited **aloud, eyes closed** on an expanding
interval. A deck that fails a mock resets to the +1wk slot.

## Intervals (Leitner-style expanding)
Learn (day 0) → +1 week → +2 weeks → +4 weeks → both mock weeks (6, 11) → Wk 12 sweep.

## Rules
1. Recite the cue-question first; only then unfold the chain. If you cannot produce
   the chain from the cue, the deck resets to +1wk.
2. Prioritize decks whose cues are **production symptoms** — the exam is
   scenario-framed; definition recall alone does not transfer.
3. In Wk 12 recite only the three flagged forget-risk verses that each deck ends with.
4. After each mock, re-weight: decks tied to missed questions move to the front.
