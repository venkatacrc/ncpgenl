# 5.18 — Contrastive loss for embeddings

**Objective:** 5.2

Embedding models are fine-tuned so similar texts are close and dissimilar far apart.
- **Triplet loss**: `max(0, d(a,p) − d(a,n) + margin)` — anchor closer to positive than
  negative by a margin.
- **InfoNCE / contrastive**: treat the matching pair as positive against in-batch
  negatives:

$$\mathcal{L}=-\log\frac{\exp(\text{sim}(a,p)/\tau)}{\sum_i \exp(\text{sim}(a,n_i)/\tau)}$$

Temperature `τ` sharpens; large in-batch negatives help. Basis of retrieval/RAG
embedding quality (2.24, 6.08).
