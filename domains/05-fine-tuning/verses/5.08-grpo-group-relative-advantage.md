# 5.08 — GRPO: group relative policy optimization

**Objective:** 5.1

GRPO (from DeepSeek-R1-style reasoning training) removes PPO's **value network**:
for each prompt it samples a **group** of `G` responses, scores them (often with a
verifiable/rule-based reward), and uses the **group mean/std as the baseline** so the
advantage is each response's reward normalized within its group:

$$A_i=\frac{r_i-\text{mean}(r_{1..G})}{\text{std}(r_{1..G})}$$

Cheaper than PPO (no value net), well-suited to reasoning tasks with automatic reward
checking.
