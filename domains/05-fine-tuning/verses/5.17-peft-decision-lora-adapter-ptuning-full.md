# 5.17 — PEFT decision: LoRA vs adapters vs P-tuning vs full FT

**Objective:** 5.2

| Method | Trains | Merge-able? | Inference cost | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full FT | all weights | n/a | none extra | highest (+ forgetting risk) |
| LoRA/QLoRA | low-rank ΔW | **yes** | zero if merged | high |
| Adapters | bottleneck layers | no | small latency | medium |
| P-tuning/prefix | soft prompt vecs | no | tiny | low–medium |

**Rule:** LoRA/QLoRA is the default (mergeable, strong, cheap). Full FT only with ample
data/compute and forgetting mitigation. P-tuning for extreme parameter efficiency /
many tasks. Adapters mostly legacy.

**Chain →** PEFT covers task adaptation; a distinct fine-tuning goal is *embeddings* —
how are those trained? (5.18)
