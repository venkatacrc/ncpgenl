# 5.04 — Reward modeling (preference pairs)

**Objective:** 5.1

A reward model (RM) scores a response's quality. Trained on human **preference pairs**
(prompt, chosen, rejected) with the Bradley-Terry loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{RM}=-\log\sigma\big(r_\theta(x,y_{chosen})-r_\theta(x,y_{rejected})\big)$$

The RM is usually the SFT model with a scalar head. It provides the reward signal PPO
optimizes. Quality/coverage of preference data caps final alignment quality.
