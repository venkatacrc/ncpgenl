# 5.15 — P-tuning / prefix tuning (continuous prompts)

**Objective:** 5.2

**Prompt tuning / P-tuning** prepend trainable **continuous embedding vectors** (soft
prompts, 2.12) to the input; the model and all real weights stay frozen — only the
prompt vectors train. **Prefix tuning** goes further: it prepends trainable vectors to
the **keys/values at every layer**, not just the input embedding, giving more capacity.
Extremely parameter-efficient (thousands of params), but usually less expressive than
LoRA for hard tasks.
