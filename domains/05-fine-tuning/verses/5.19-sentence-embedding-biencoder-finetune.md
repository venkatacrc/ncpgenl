# 5.19 — Sentence-embedding fine-tuning (bi-encoder)

**Objective:** 5.2

A **bi-encoder** encodes query and document **separately** into vectors compared by
cosine — fast, precomputable, the backbone of retrieval/RAG. Contrast the
**cross-encoder**, which jointly encodes a (query, doc) pair for a relevance score —
more accurate but O(n) per query, used for **re-ranking** the bi-encoder's top-k.
Fine-tune bi-encoders with contrastive loss (5.18) on (query, positive, negatives).
NVIDIA: **NeMo Retriever** embedding + reranking models.
