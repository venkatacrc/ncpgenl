# 5.09 — GRPO vs PPO vs DPO decision

**Objective:** 5.1

| Method | Reward model? | Value net? | Data | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DPO | no (implicit) | no | fixed preference pairs | cheap, stable preference alignment |
| PPO | yes (learned) | yes | online rollouts | rich/complex rewards, exploration |
| GRPO | reward fn (often rule-based) | **no** | grouped rollouts | reasoning/verifiable rewards, cheaper RL |

Escalate DPO → GRPO → PPO as reward complexity and need for online exploration rise.

**Chain →** Alignment methods update behavior; but most of us can't afford full FT — how
do we adapt parameter-efficiently? (5.10)
