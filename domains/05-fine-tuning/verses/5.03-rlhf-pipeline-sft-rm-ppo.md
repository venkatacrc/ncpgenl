# 5.03 — RLHF pipeline (SFT → RM → PPO)

**Objective:** 5.1

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback aligns models to preferences in three
stages: (1) **SFT** base; (2) train a **reward model** (5.04) on human preference
comparisons; (3) **RL-optimize** the policy against the reward model with **PPO**
(5.05), regularized by a KL penalty to the SFT reference. Powerful but complex: four
models in memory (policy, reference, reward, value), unstable, expensive.
