# 5.07 — DPO vs RLHF/PPO tradeoffs

**Objective:** 5.1

- **DPO**: simpler, stable, cheaper (no RM/value net, no online sampling), needs a fixed
  preference dataset. Can overfit to the dataset and is off-policy.
- **PPO/RLHF**: on-policy exploration via a reward model can exceed the static dataset
  and optimize non-differentiable/complex rewards, but is unstable, memory-heavy, and
  hard to tune.

**Rule:** default to DPO for preference alignment; use PPO when you need online
exploration, a learned reward covering many behaviors, or reward signals you can't bake
into pairs.
