# 5.21 — Overfitting signals & regularization

**Objective:** 5.3

Signal: training loss keeps falling while **validation loss rises** (diverging curves).
Regularizers: **weight decay** (L2), **dropout**, **data augmentation**, smaller
LoRA rank, fewer epochs, and more/diverse data. For LLM fine-tuning, 1–3 epochs on
instruction data is often enough; more epochs memorize. Label smoothing softens
over-confidence.
