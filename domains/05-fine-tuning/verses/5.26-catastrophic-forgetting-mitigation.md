# 5.26 — Catastrophic forgetting & mitigation

**Objective:** 5.4

Fine-tuning on a narrow task can overwrite general capabilities — **catastrophic
forgetting**. Mitigations: **PEFT** (LoRA freezes the base, structurally limiting drift),
lower LR / fewer epochs, **replay** (mix in general/pretraining data), regularization
toward the base (EWC, KL to reference — the same mechanism as RLHF's KL penalty, 5.05),
and multi-task instead of sequential training. PEFT's inherent resistance to forgetting
is a key reason it's the default.

**Chain →** Fine-tuning done; the next question is rigorous measurement — **Domain 6:
Evaluation.**
