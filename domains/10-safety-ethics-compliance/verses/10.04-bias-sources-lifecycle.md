# 10.04 — Sources of bias across the lifecycle

**Objective:** 10.2

Bias enters at many stages: **data** (historical/representation/sampling bias, skewed
web corpora), **labeling** (annotator bias), **algorithmic** (objective amplifies
majority), **evaluation** (unrepresentative benchmarks), and **deployment** (feedback
loops, 9.10). Because it compounds, mitigation must span the lifecycle, not just a
post-hoc filter. Identifying the *source* dictates the *fix* (data vs training vs
guardrail).
