# 2.09 — Few-shot CoT

**Objective:** 2.1

Provide k exemplars that each include the **reasoning trace**, not just the answer.
Teaches the model the *style* and *granularity* of reasoning for the domain. Best for
smaller models or specialized formats where zero-shot CoT (2.07) underperforms.
Exemplar quality dominates: one wrong trace can anchor the model to a bad pattern.
