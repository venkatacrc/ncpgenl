# 1.07 — Encoder-decoder (cross-attention)

**Objective:** 1.1

Seq2seq models (T5, BART, original Transformer) pair a **bidirectional encoder** with
a **causal decoder** joined by **cross-attention**: the decoder supplies `Q`, the
encoder's final states supply `K,V`. Decoding is thus conditioned on a
fully-contextualized source — the natural fit for translation and summarization where
source/target lengths and vocabularies differ. Cost: you pay for both stacks plus
cross-attention.

```python
# decoder layer has THREE attention calls:
h = h + self_attn(ln1(h), mask=causal)          # 1: causal self-attn
h = h + cross_attn(ln2(h), kv=encoder_states)   # 2: Q=decoder, K,V=encoder
h = h + ffn(ln3(h))                             # 3: FFN
```

**Chain →** All three architectures emit hidden states we call embeddings — but what
is an embedding, precisely? (1.08)
