# 3.11 — Splits & leakage prevention

**Objective:** 3.2

Split into train/val/test. Prevent leakage: deduplicate **across** splits (3.08),
split by **group** (user/document/time) when records are correlated, and **decontaminate**
training data against benchmark/eval sets (a duplicate of a test item in train inflates
scores). Fit all preprocessing (scalers, tokenizers, filters) on **train only**. Time-
series/production data → temporal split, never random.
