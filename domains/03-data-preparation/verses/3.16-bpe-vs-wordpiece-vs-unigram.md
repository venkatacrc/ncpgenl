# 3.16 — BPE vs WordPiece vs Unigram/SentencePiece

**Objective:** 3.3

- **BPE**: greedy frequency merges; ordered merge list; byte-level variant = no OOV.
  GPT/Llama/RoBERTa.
- **WordPiece**: likelihood-maximizing merges; `##` continuations; greedy
  longest-match. BERT.
- **Unigram LM**: starts with a large vocab and *prunes* to maximize corpus likelihood;
  probabilistic segmentation. T5/ALBERT.
- **SentencePiece**: a *library/framework* (not an algorithm) implementing BPE or
  Unigram directly on raw text (treats space as a symbol `▁`), language-agnostic, no
  pre-tokenization needed.

**Chain →** Choosing an algorithm still leaves the vocab-size knob — how is a tokenizer
trained and sized? (3.17)
