# 6.06 — LLM-as-a-judge

**Objective:** 6.1

Use a strong LLM to score/compare outputs against a rubric (pointwise score or
**pairwise** preference). Scales far cheaper than humans and correlates reasonably for
open-ended quality. **Known biases to control**: position bias (favors first/second —
randomize order), verbosity bias (favors longer), self-preference (favors its own
family), and leniency. Mitigate with clear rubrics, reference answers, order swapping,
and calibration against human labels.
