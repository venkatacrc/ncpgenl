# 9.04 — Logging, tracing & observability

**Objective:** 9.2

The three pillars: **metrics** (aggregate trends), **logs** (discrete events — prompts,
errors, guardrail hits, with PII handling), **traces** (a request's path across services
— tokenizer → retriever → model, via OpenTelemetry). Together they enable root-cause
analysis. For LLMs, log prompt/response metadata (not raw PII), latencies per stage, and
token counts. Correlate by request/trace ID.

**Chain →** Logs are noisy — how do we surface the anomalies automatically? (9.05)
