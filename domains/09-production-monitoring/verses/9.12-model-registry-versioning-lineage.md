# 9.12 — Model registry, versioning & lineage

**Objective:** 9.4

A **model registry** tracks every model version with its **lineage**: training data
snapshot, code/commit, hyperparameters, eval results, and stage (staging/production/
archived). Enables reproducibility, auditability, and one-click rollback. Distinct from
the *serving* version policy (8.18): the registry is the source of truth; the model repo
is what's deployed. Tag artifacts immutably.

**Chain →** All this exists to keep the service trustworthy and up — how do we frame
that? (9.13)
